## Residual permission: corrected reference, retained limits

The [A05 topology](../a05/README.md) and extracted Rev.1 pads/BOM establish
SAFE_HI/LO → 3×560 kΩ → SENSE_HI/LO, 27 kΩ/10 nF returns to **VMID**, and a
94 kΩ differential bleeder. A floating OPEN or uncharged passive resistor has
both terminals near VMID, not GND. The relevant departure is
`Vterminal−VMID = (Vsense−VMID) × (1.680M+27k)/27k`.

Policy uses `max(|VHI−VMID|, |VLO−VMID|, |VHI−VLO|)`. It retains release ≤0.75 V,
block ≥1.0 V, existing hysteresis, eight consecutive SAFE evaluations, ADC clipping,
valid VMID (1.35–1.95 V), freshness (permit residual ≤20 ms/battery ≤1000 ms), charger,
battery, fault and single-use K1 authorization. Absolute terminal diagnostics are
retained; their ~1.65 V idle values are no longer misinterpreted as residual energy.
Nonfinite policy input cannot retain SAFE. B01 rejects contradictory SAFE/count data.

Real auxiliary ADC conversion/policy and permit regressions cover passive bias at
VMID 1.40/1.65/1.90 V, ±1.25 V asymmetric and common-mode departures, and opposite
±0.65 V departures (1.30 V differential). Existing saturation, invalid VMID, reset,
freshness, charger and conversion-fault tests remain. The 16-case metrology regression
uses real sensor/range/K1/permit/FSM/DSP components, a delayed 400 ms injected DMA
completion and no persisted calibration; only peripheral observations are mocked.
It covers clipped HG→1X, both invalid paths, grounded terminals, timeout, cancellation,
K1 write failure, ADC acquisition failure and an active safety fault. NTC temperature remains metadata,
not a new thermal permit; failed ADC conversions remain blocking faults.

**Protection limits:** this senses departures from circuit VMID, not protective
chassis/earth. An externally driven equal potential matching VMID is indistinguishable
from natural passive bias; it is not independently detected. A floating circuit's
potential relative to earth is not measured. Below-threshold voltage, energized AC
whose timing/bandwidth/averaging aliases the residual samples, unseen spikes and
stored energy are not ruled out by SAFE. Offset, divider tolerances and ADC rail scale
remain uncharacterized. No energized DUT, mains, charged capacitor or CAT claim.

## Large/low-impedance DUTs: HOLD and abort correction

Previously abort commanded OFF while range and K1 remained connected across separate
steps. OFF drives PA8 LOW, making filtered VEXC tend toward GND instead of VMID.
The software now stops ADC, requests **range OFF → K1 SAFE → excitation OFF** in the
same bounded cleanup step. It attempts all actions on failure, latches failures and
retains the 8 ms release guard before auxiliary resume. No neutral-ramp delay weakens
emergency shutdown. Successful shutdown retains its existing neutral/contact guards.

This improves command order, not a guarantee of physical isolation. MOSFET turn-off,
relay release/bounce and capacitance must be measured. Excitation DMA ISR, reset and
power loss can still force OFF before cooperative isolation; that fail-safe is retained.
Failure to disable a range or relay cannot be declared physically SAFE by software.

Nominal worst-case ground-directed step from VMID plus source peak is ~1.79 V at
100-class excitation before filter effects. Into a near-zero DUT through 10 Ω this
predicts ~179 mA **ideal transient demand**, not available/rated MCP6002 current.
Matched 1k/1k first resistor demand is below 1 mA. Real output limits, RON, series
resistors, coils and clamp/3V3 injection dominate faults. No firmware current sensor
or validated energy limit is invented.

10 mF with the 94 kΩ bleed alone has τ=940 s. Including the two 1.707 MΩ
sensing branches as a 3.414 MΩ differential return gives nominal R≈91.48 kΩ,
τ≈914.8 s and ~1,735 s from 5 V to 0.75 V (stable VMID, no other leakage);
0.75 V still stores ~2.8 mJ. SAFE is not a discharged-capacitor certificate.
Polarized electrolytics can experience reverse AC and unknown DC offsets. **Large,
charged and polarized-capacitor experiments remain ELECTRICAL_SAFETY_HOLD** pending
separate waveform/polarity/current review. Firmware cannot establish DUT energy before
first excitation. No permanent BOM/wire-up repair is prescribed without bench evidence.

## Host-only required-domain matrix

[63 synthetic cases](feasibility.csv), reproduced by the existing
`wtk_measurement_dsp_test`, cover every requested L/C value at all three frequencies.
An independent double-precision divider and loaded-ladder oracle chooses the closest
logarithmic RREF, 100 mV on 10 Ω/500 mV on others, and predicts source/RET/HG peak,
current, ADC-code span and formula observability. The table is **not** an operator
permission table. Matrix categories: 26 PLAUSIBLE_FOR_BENCH_TEST, 12 LOW_CONFIDENCE,
16 OUTSIDE_DEMONSTRATED_CAPABILITY, 9 ELECTRICAL_SAFETY_HOLD.

Numerical discrepancy vs double oracle: maximum relative X error `1.66555334e-7`;
L/C unit-conversion error `1.16211595e-7`. Neither is instrument accuracy. The oracle
excludes physical ADC noise, tolerances, ESR/DCR, parasitics and loading. At 500 nH/
10 kHz, X≈0.0314 Ω; favorable 10 Ω range gives ~0.228 mV 1X/~3.52 mV HG under the
nominal ladder model (about 0.28/4.37 peak ADC codes). Lead inductance/contact loss and
SHORT repeatability can dominate. At 1 pF/10 kHz, X≈15.9 MΩ; the 1 MΩ range is still
near OPEN and pF fixture/probe leakage dominates. These endpoints are not qualified.

The existing 3×5.1k/1nF loaded ladder gives H amplitude/phase: 10 Hz 0.999998665/
−0.11016°, 1 kHz 0.986906/−10.9362°, 10 kHz 0.512455/−75.5608°. At 10 Hz the quantized
fundamental predicts ~101.079/498.933 mVrms at nominal 3.3 V before buffer uncertainty;
1/10 kHz predictions remain in the [baseline](measurement-baseline.md).
Duty quantization, zero-order hold, buffer response and DMA start affect actual phase/
distortion. Measure real VEXC−VMID; do not compensate PWM from these predictions.
