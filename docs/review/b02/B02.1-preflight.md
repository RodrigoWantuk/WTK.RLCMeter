## Demonstrated defect and minimal correction

`hw_metrology_measure` consumed a permit, paused auxiliary sensing, then requested
K1 using a new sensor snapshot. Real `hw_aux_sensors_pause()` immediately invalidates
residual evidence. Consequently that snapshot is UNKNOWN and K1 authorization fails,
even when the original permit was valid. Earlier metrology mocks did not invalidate
residual evidence on pause. The added real-component regression fails before the fix.

The correction changes three existing transitions: validate permit → authorize K1
with fresh sensor evidence → pause auxiliary ADC → existing K1 operate guard.
There is no cached permission, threshold change or bypass. The guard deadline remains
10 ms from K1 command; metrology ADC acquisition follows pause and that guard.
Dynamic charger/range/fault checks and the 8 ms release guard remain. Cancellation
between K1 request and pause is tested as well as cancellation during acquisition.
No GPIO, ADC/DMA, excitation, calibration, profile or persistent-layout change.

## Unchanged residual gate blocks the predicted floating fixture

[A05 circuit evidence](../a05/README.md) establishes each SAFE terminal → 3×560 kΩ
→ SENSE, with 27 kΩ/10 nF from SENSE to VMID. The two SAFE terminals have a series
94 kΩ bleeder between them, not a ground return. K1 SAFE isolates its RET/VMID
MEASURE contacts. An uncharged floating resistor or OPEN therefore tends toward
VMID, nominally 1.65 V, on both SAFE and SENSE nodes.

`hw_residual_terminal_voltage()` reconstructs absolute terminal voltage as
`VMID + (SENSE - VMID) × 63.2222`. Equal SENSE/VMID therefore reconstructs 1.65 V.
`hw_residual_policy_evaluate()` requires both absolute terminal magnitudes and their
difference ≤0.75 V for eight consecutive evaluations; either magnitude ≥1.0 V blocks.
The real auxiliary-policy/permit regression rejects these nominal floating readings
before K1 or DMA. This is a source-derived discrepancy, not a measured board result.

The gate remains unchanged. Referencing individual terminals to VMID would change
its accepted absolute voltage window and needs a separate reviewed safety decision.
Do not ground TEST_LO, falsify sensor readings, force K1 or use raw capture to evade
this blocker. No electrical workaround is approved here.

## Exact legitimate authorization conditions

- Correct HSE/PLL clock: 72 MHz SYSCLK, 12 MHz ADC; no HSI fallback. Request parsing
  accepts `lab metrology measure 100 100 1k`; 100 Hz/100 mVrms/1 kΩ is supported.
- Charger ABSENT: no VBUS on J_PWR.4, PA15 LOW through its real network; read failure
  is UNKNOWN and blocks. Firmware absence does not prove hardware coil inhibition.
- VMID valid (1.35–1.95 V), residual SAFE after eight evaluations, non-saturated ADC;
  residual evidence age ≤20 ms at permit issue. Public sensor freshness permits
  50 ms, which alone is insufficient for a measurement permit.
- Battery OK or LOW, age ≤1000 ms; CRITICAL/UNKNOWN blocks. ≤3.25 V is CRITICAL,
  ≤3.50 V is LOW, >4.35 V is UNKNOWN. No active safety fault or conflicting operation.
- The B02 removable 5 V → 10 kΩ → VBAT → 33 kΩ → GND fixture, including the board's
  100 kΩ/100 kΩ divider load, predicts VBAT 3.6954 V and ADC_BAT 1.8477 V. Quantized
  raw observations pass real battery conversion/policy. Measure both nodes first,
  retain BENCH_SENSE_SIMULATION provenance and allow ≥2 s refresh; never feed 5 V
  directly into VBAT/PA6. Unavailable/failed conversions block.
- Nominal 100 kΩ NTC/top resistor gives about 1.65 V and 25 °C. Invalid NTC raw data
  yields unavailable temperature; temperature validity is **not** currently a permit
  input. An ADC conversion failure does latch a blocking fault. No thermal gate added.
- Session selects 1 kΩ/address 2 through real disable/address/dead-time/settle logic;
  RANGE_READY must match, K1 initially SAFE. A disabled range before the session is
  expected. Single-use permit TTL remains 5 ms; the operator cannot issue GPIO permits.

## Real command/result path and OPEN

Parser → clock/request validation → range FSM → neutral/quiet → sensor-backed permit
→ K1 permission → auxiliary pause → relay guard → ADC ownership → sine settling →
packed dual-ADC DMA → teardown → raw statistics/DSP → cooperative UART publication.
At 100 Hz: 80 ms sine settle, 256 sample instants at 6400 Hz (40 ms block), three
packed words each. RET quality selects usable HG or valid 1X. Complex correction and
classification use existing C code, not a second measurement implementation.

The blank BRINGUP diagnostic allows ideal fallback. Successful fixed-command output
contains `DSP_BEGIN v=1`, `calibration=MISSING source=IDEAL`, `dsp_status=OK`,
`return_channel=RET_HG` or `RET_1X`, `z_real_mohm`, `z_imag_mohm`, `z_mag_mohm`,
`phase_mrad`, `interpretation=RESISTIVE`, `DSP_END`. Automatic-session reporting calls
its equivalent source `IDEAL_UNQUALIFIED`. Neither is persisted/qualified OSL.
Numerical UART publication follows K1 SAFE/release, excitation OFF, range OFF and
ADC restoration. GPIO command success is not contact feedback.

`lab cal acquire open 10k 100 1k` is accepted: OPEN, 10 kHz, 100 mVrms, 1 kΩ RREF.
Its calibration session reuses the same acquisition and safety machinery, so it
also remains blocked by the residual issue. OPEN uses finite admittance evidence;
failed/canceled/clipped unusable samples cannot complete an accepted observation.
Valid 1X with unusable HG can be fitted by the PC solver; a new regression verifies
this and rejection when neither path is usable. HG characterization still needs
actual overlap evidence. OPEN at this condition remains a separate Y04/headroom
bench approval after measured excitation; its syntax is not electrical approval.
`lab cal cancel` exists. Fixed `lab metrology measure` has timeouts/internal abort,
but no dedicated text cancel command; `lab measure cancel` is a different session.
