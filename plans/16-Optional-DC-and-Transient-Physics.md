## 1. Distinguish physical quantities explicitly
- **Inductor winding DCR R_DC (Ω):** ohmic copper/connection resistance with controlled low-voltage DC, ideally settled, bias/polarity/temperature specified. NOT the same as frequency-dependent equivalent AC series resistance (AC winding loss).
- **Inductor current/flux step response:** ideal series RL rise/decay time constant tau_L=L/(R_DCR+R_external+R_source+other_series). For an inductor the stored field is MAGNETIC and energy E_L=1/2 L I²; the field does not 'reabsorb electric field'. tau_L is a property of a *specific loop*, not a universal intrinsic property independent of load.
- **Capacitor effective RC discharge with specified known load:** tau_C=(R_load_parallel_equivalent)*C for simple first-order approximation. This can be predicted from C and an actual R_load or tested with a step transient. Label external load and frequency/bias dependence of the capacitance; do not mislabel it as intrinsic self-discharge. The intrinsic initial stored energy E_C=1/2 C V².
- **Capacitor intrinsic self-discharge/leakage:** after a declared safe charge bias V0 and wait, any leakage current, insulation/leakage resistance R_leak≈V/I and/or effective decay tau_self. Real capacitors may show voltage-dependent leakage, dielectric absorption, humidity, test-rig parallel loads and nonexponential relaxation. Neither a 2-point decay slope nor one R value can universally characterize it.
- **Capacitor dielectric absorption/recovery:** voltage rebound after a controlled charge/discharge/disconnect sequence, sometimes expressed as a measured % of pre-discharge voltage over a stated period. Different from leakage and ESR; Rev.1 may be unable to isolate/re-observe the capacitor without loading.
- **Capacitor charging-speed under controlled source:** test-rig-dependent tau=(R_source+R_series)*C and nonlinear source/relay/filter. Capacitor 'how quickly can discharge' depends on load and operating voltage, not only on C.

## 2. Actual Rev.1 signal paths and why intrinsic leakage is challenging
H16-01. In K1 SAFE: TEST_HI -> SAFE_HI; TEST_LO -> SAFE_LO; the safety circuit has two series 47k resistors between terminals, nominal 94kΩ. An attached charged capacitor is discharged through this resistor. Tau_SAFE ≈94,000Ω*C in ideal approximation: for 1µF ~94ms, for 100µF ~9.4s. A SAFE raw residual-voltage decay is dominated by this **instrument-imposed external load** whenever DUT leakage resistance is much greater than 94kΩ. Therefore label 'SAFE fixture discharge τ', never 'capacitor natural leakage τ'. High-voltage behavior outside the qualified envelope remains prohibited.
H16-02. In K1 MEASURE: TEST_LO -> VMID, TEST_HI -> RET; selected RREF connects RET through switching devices to the actively buffered VEXC source. The path can actively source/discharge an attached DUT and also loads it. To measure intrinsic open-circuit self-discharge, test whether **range disabled + K1 MEASURE** could leave RET sensed by U5A high-impedance with no RREF path; currently the authorization FSM normally requires a selected ready range before energizing K1. This would need a separately reviewed safe, bounded high-Z permit and termination strategy; it is not a ready firmware feature. Input op-amp bias, RREF switch off leakage, PCB contamination, ADC impedance/protection and the fixture itself must be characterized. Never bypass K1 safety merely to read an open-circuit capacitor.
H16-03. Published safety residual-voltage network senses in SAFE through high-value dividers but the 94kΩ bleed remains present. DNP guard/TVS may affect leakage, but do not populate as a shortcut without a measured and modeled A/B comparison; added protection can worsen small-C/high-Z precision.
H16-04. Current DC pilot: BRINGUP only, 1MΩ RREF, static CCR81 vs center 80, approx 20.625mV nominal step, observed source ceiling 50mV, 2ms nominal settling and existing 256 instants @64ksps. It cannot substantiate low-Ω DCR. An AC preflight does not rule out a DC inductor short. Lower RREF, additional bias or polarity reversal require current limit, source amplifier power and instrument protection sign-off.
H16-05. Source excitation filter comprises three 5.1kΩ/1nF stages. In the high-level picture each stage has ~5.1µs RC, so source/filter response is a significant dynamic delay relative to tens-of-µs coil transients. The MCP6002 closed-loop response, switching MOSFETs, ADC rank skew and actual fixture circuit further constrain observability. Treat this as an engineering caution and measure actual step response rather than assuming an exact composite 15.3µs constant.
H16-06. ADC sample spacing is 6.25µs at 160k sample instants/sec (10kHz AC profile) or 15.625µs at 64k sample instants/sec (1kHz/DC pilot profile). The 256-sample block lasts ~1.6ms or ~4ms respectively. Existing DMA/trigger profiles are AC-coherent, not optimized single-shot transient recorders. A meaningful time-domain model requires a deterministic step-edge timestamp, simultaneous ADC/reference behavior and a recorded acquisition period/trigger; the scope can investigate feasibility before MCU support is added.

## 3. Feasibility tiers: prioritize within hardware limits
| Parameter | First deliverable | How to measure/derive | Confidence and owner go/no-go |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DC winding resistance (Ω) | BRINGUP test -> optional PRODUCT | safely authorized RREF/DUT current; measured DC Vsource/return/VMID, blank SHORT compensation; repeat fixture reseats | medium effort but low-ohm may be unresolved; require explicit smallest measurable Ω and error floor |
| Capacitor charge/discharge under known load (s, tau) | initially PC calculator / scope research | measured C, declared source/load R; optional safe step measurement and exponential fit | low implementation cost as **calculated fixture-specific value**; requires model and known resistor to call measured |
| Intrinsic capacitor leakage/self-discharge (A, Rleak, time) | feasibility only | quantify TRUE disconnected open circuit or effective high-Z sense, remove 94k SAFE and 1M RREF effects, known DC bias and long dwell | difficult for Rev1; likely defer to next PCB without usable high-Z switch path |
| Dielectric absorption / voltage rebound (%) | feasibility only | charge, discharge, isolate, high-Z re-observation without bleed and measure rebound against initial voltage | very difficult with permanent SAFE bleeder and relay; do not promise for Rev1 |
| Inductor current/field decay tau_L | PC-derived if DCR+L trustworthy, plus scope investigation | tau=L/(R_DC+known loop R); time-domain current step across measured shunt if valid | easy as **calculated specified-loop estimate**, difficult as stand-alone intrinsic measurement; scope verifies bandwidth |
| Stored energies | PC optional calculated E=1/2 CV² and E=1/2 LI² | requires user-supplied intended voltage/current; RLC alone is insufficient | derived engineering calculator, not independently measured DUT energy |

Do not confuse a virtual displayed reference formula with a new physically measured parameter. Qualify labels as CALCULATED versus MEASURED versus ESTIMATED.

## 5. Calibration data and UI semantics
- Keep independent records for DC offset/SHORT contact and known resistor; don't reuse an AC OSL calibration as a DC calibration.
- For leakage, require fixed bias, elapsed dwell, temperature, humidity where possible, net instrument loading and final confidence. If leakage is below fixture error floor, report upper/lower bound or n/a as mathematically justified — never 0Ω or infinity by overflow.
- For derived RC/RL time constant, always state effective load/source resistance R and whether L/C is at 100Hz/1k/10k; term is conditional on model and environmental variables.
- Hantek timebase accuracy can help establish τ from observed slopes, but scope input/probe loading and absolute gain/offset matter; an exp fit may be timing-accurate yet assign the wrong τ to a loaded DUT. Never claim the scope can calibrate every parameter or remove 1% resistor uncertainty from a voltage-divider impedance result.
- STORE all waveforms, graphical fits, standard inventory and temperature in PC workbench. On target only persist qualified fixed-point/float compact coefficients and no raw time-history UI.

## 6. Stage gates and stopping rules
G1 (pre-solder): approve DNP/conditional assembly and mark net discrepancies; no physical measurements declared.
G2 (bench AC): validate safe K1/range, source/ADC and primary RLC. Extended DC experiments may run only after this baseline is stable.
G3 (DC current): signed low-voltage current/dissipation/offset limits and a suitable reference range; one 1M exploratory pilot is not DCR.
G4 (fast transient): verified stimulus rise time, ADC/scope timebase, physically allowed current and DUT safe discharge.
G5 (intrinsic self-discharge): demonstrably isolate DUT from 94k SAFE shunt and quantify sensor loading below desired leakage floor; otherwise defer.
G6 (PRODUCT): independent held-outs, owner-approved error statement, small linked footprint compatible with dynamic full-product Flash budget; optional features may remain PC-only.

## 7. Explicit non-goals
No arbitrary high voltage, no battery/charged capacitor connect without residual validation, no external voltage injection into DUT for convenience, no differential probing by floating the scope's protective earth, no catalog ESR or L field lifetime based only on 3-point AC tests, no forced last-minute coding tricks to fit internal Flash.
